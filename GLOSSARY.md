# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Staff**:
The people who run Cadence. Staff decide Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Team (a Team is a customer organisation that buys seats), Admin, Support

**Team Seat**:
One place on a course, bought by a Team for one of its people. A Team Seat is unclaimed until someone redeems it. A claimed Team Seat can't be refunded, and unclaimed Team Seats stop working when their purchase is refunded.
_Avoid_: Coupon, Licence

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. Staff approve or decline it. The decision is final. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request Staff haven't decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Closed Refund Request**:
A Refund Request Staff have decided: either approved or declined.
_Avoid_: Decided, Completed, Resolved

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request Staff have accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund, and it ends the learner's access to the course straight away, whatever happens to the money.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request Staff have turned down, with a note telling the learner why. It has no Refund, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund Staff have approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.
